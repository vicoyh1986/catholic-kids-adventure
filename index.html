A little faith trail for our island home

Love grows when we notice our neighbours.

Singapore's Catholic family is beautifully diverse. In our homes, schools, parishes, playgrounds, and shared neighbourhoods, we can reflect Jesus through kind words, patient hearts, and care for the people God places near us.

For catechists and families

A flexible 30-minute faith-sharing guide

Use a story, a Beatitude, and one small mission to help children connect Sunday faith with everyday life.

  1. 1
    Gather (3 min)

    Make the Sign of the Cross and welcome each child by name.

  2. 2
    Listen (8 min)

    Open one parable or Beatitude and invite children to notice what Jesus does or says.

  3. 3
    Wonder (6 min)

    Use the conversation prompt above. Accept short answers and give everyone a chance to speak.

  4. 4
    Act (8 min)

    Choose one Singapore Faith Trail mission that children can try safely with a parent, teacher, or catechist.

  5. 5
    Pray and send (5 min)

    Pray the island-home prayer together, then send children out with one kind action to remember.

For parish use, follow your parish's safeguarding practice and formation plan.
